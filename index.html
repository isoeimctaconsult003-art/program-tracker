    Travel: live GPS, distance, speed and stops

    Your travel starts from where you are now. Add the places you will visit, press Start, and the page records your route, distance and speed. A stop is logged by itself when you stay within 30 m for more than 5 minutes. When you end the travel it is saved to your Google Sheet.
